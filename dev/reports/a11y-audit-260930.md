# Accessibility audit, 30 September 2026

Target: WCAG 2.2 level AA. Scope covered in this pass: the three standalone pieces (Superior Ice Year, Meridian Chorus, Water Year Rings), plus a source read of the Climate Pair script and the Superior Ice wrapper page. The Quarto pages were not built, so they were not tested (see "Not checked").

Authorship: written by Claude (a cloud session) from tests it ran on the repository at commit `5ecf835`. Nothing here was checked with a screen reader, Safari or a phone. Findings marked "verified" were reproduced with the commands in `dev/a11y/`. Findings marked "judgement" are my reading, and Graham should decide them.

## TL;DR

- **The base is better than the brief feared.** Every control on the three pages can be reached and used from the keyboard, nothing traps focus, and the pages reflow at 320 px with no sideways scroll. A keyboard user can play, scrub, pin a region and let go.
- **The main gap is meaning, not operation.** A blind or low-vision user can operate the pages but gets little of what the pictures say. The five worst problems, in order:
  1. **A11Y-01.** Each Superior Ice region button announces only its name, not its ice percentage or water temperature for the day.
  2. **A11Y-02.** On Water Year Rings, a ring's value for a day appears only when a pointer hovers or clicks it.
  3. **A11Y-03.** The Water Year score (the piece's notation) has no label, and on a phone its scrolling box cannot be reached by keyboard.
  4. **A11Y-04.** The seek bars read out raw numbers ("170") instead of a date.
  5. **A11Y-05.** Pinning a region changes the text panel but announces nothing.
- **Missing `lang` on all three pages (A11Y-06)** is a one-line fix with real effect: screen readers pick the wrong voice and pronunciation without it.
- **Six small-text colour pairs fail the 4.5:1 contrast rule** (A11Y-07). Those are palette choices, so they wait for Graham's A/B decision.
- **What I did not change.** This pass is findings only. The fixes listed as "safe" alter no look or sound. I have not applied any of them.

### Detail and proposed fixes

**A11Y-01. Region values are not announced.** Each region row is `role="button"` with `aria-label="Western Arm: show its season"`. The label replaces the row's contents, and the contents of a button are not read separately. The accessibility tree in Playwright shows the name as the label alone, while the visible text ("D5 · 91.0°W 47.0°N 3% 3.6°") sits inside it. Those numbers change every frame as the piece plays. Also, the visible numbers have no units, so "3% 3.6°" never says ice or water.
- Repro: `node dev/a11y/tree.js` and read the region buttons.
- Fix (safe, S): keep the short label, add `aria-describedby` pointing at a visually hidden sentence per region, updated when the user focuses the row or pins it, for example "Western Arm, ice 3 percent, water 3.6 degrees Celsius". Do not update it every frame.
- Meridian uses the same row pattern (`aria-label="…: show its year"`). I did not inspect its values, so treat it as likely, not verified.

**A11Y-02. Water Year ring is pointer-only.** The canvas label says "hover a ring for its value that day, click to jump to that date". A keyboard user has no equivalent, and a touch user has no hover. The event list under the score (about 30 buttons, for example "Ice forms on the Granby (Nov 25)") is a real text alternative for the key events, but not for daily values.
- Fix (S to M): offer the same per-day readout as A11Y-01 for the ring, driven by the seek bar's day, and reword the label so it doesn't promise hover. A daily data table is the fuller alternative (M).

**A11Y-03. Score canvas.** `#score` has no label. At 390 px the box that scrolls it has no focusable content, so a keyboard user cannot scroll it (axe `scrollable-region-focusable`).
- Fix (safe, XS): give the canvas `role="img"` and a label saying it is the piece's notation and that the event list carries the same events in text. Give the scrolling box `tabindex="0"` with a label so it can be scrolled with arrow keys.

**A11Y-04. Slider values.** All three seek bars are `input type=range` with 0–1000 and no `aria-valuetext`, so the value read out is the raw number (Superior shows "170" at day 41).
- Fix (safe, S): set `aria-valuetext` to the date and elapsed time already shown on screen ("Dec 11, 2025, day 41 of 227"), updated on `input` and on change, not on every animation frame.

**A11Y-05. No announcement when a region is pinned.** Superior's only live region is the roll-call caption (`#rollCue`, polite). `setFocus` rewrites `#focusT` and `#focusK` (the region's name, notes, peak and event counts) with no announcement.
- Fix (safe, XS): make `#focusK` (or a hidden copy) `aria-live="polite"`, and update it only when a region is pinned or released, never during playback.

**A11Y-06. `lang`.** The pages begin `<!doctype html><meta charset…>` with no `<html>` attribute (axe `html-has-lang`). Fix (safe, XS): `<html lang="en">` on each. Consider `lang="en-CA"` if Graham prefers, since he writes Canadian English.

**A11Y-07. Small-text contrast.** Measured by axe; failing pairs only:

| Ratio | Text on ground | Page | Example element | Nodes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.31:1 | `#455455` on `#0c1618` | Water Year | `#mode-0 .name` | 3 |
| 2.94:1 | `#5b6878` on `#1d1e1d` | Meridian | `div[data-i=11] .place small` | 2 |
| 3.33:1 | `#5b6878` on `#0b1119` | Meridian | `#legend` | 14 |
| 3.43:1 | `#56707f` on `#0c1822` | Superior | `.nm small` (region coordinates) | 10 |
| 3.64:1 | `#5d7374` on `#0c1618` | Water Year | `.dnote` | 1 |
| 3.71:1 | `#56707f` on `#060e15` | Superior | `.lk` (overlay label) | 11 |

The rule needs 4.5:1 for text this size (about 8–9 pt). The 2.31:1 case may be a deliberately inactive state (I did not check), which WCAG exempts, so confirm before changing it. **For GW**, because it alters the palette: a small A/B pair per page (current grey against a lighter grey chosen to reach 4.5:1) is the smallest useful decision. Focus rings, large text and the data colours were not measured (see "Not checked").

**A11Y-08. Map canvas focus stop.** `#over` has `tabindex="0"` but handles only `mousemove`, `mouseleave` and `click` (`superior-ice/index.html` lines 574–576). Arrow keys and Enter change nothing (tested). The region list right below is the working keyboard path, so nobody is blocked; the stop just adds a Tab press that does nothing.
- Fix (safe, XS): remove `tabindex`, add `role="img"`, and end the label with "The region list below has the same information."

**A11Y-09. Landmarks.** axe reports no `main` landmark and content outside landmarks (Superior 14 nodes, Meridian 7, Water Year 15). The pages are short, so a skip link is not required, but a `<main>` is cheap. Fix (safe, XS): wrap the content in `<main>`.

**A11Y-10. Layers table.** The header row has four columns (`<th></th>` is empty) and each body row has three cells. Fix (safe, XS): delete the empty `<th>`.

**A11Y-11. No prose account of the season (judgement).** What exists: the Layers table (what each instrument means), the roll call with live captions, a one-line caption, a sentence under the timeline ("Lake-wide ice peaked at 62.6 % on Mar 2, 2026…"), and the region readouts once A11Y-01 is fixed. What is missing is a short spoken-order narrative of the four movements: when the ice arrives, where, what the ear should notice. Whether that is needed under 1.2.1 for a sonification is arguable, since the sound is the art. It is the piece for a blind user who wants the picture's story, and for a deaf user who wants the sound's. I would generate it from the piece's own data JSON so it cannot drift, and put the wording to Graham, since it is written in the site's voice. **For GW.**

## What is already working (verified)

- **Keyboard walk** (`dev/a11y/walk.js`): Superior 28 tab stops, Meridian 23, Water Year 33. Each is reachable in a logical order, none traps focus, and each has a computed outline or shadow (whether it looks visible enough was not judged).
- **Names:** controls have accessible names, including the roll-call button, the overlay toggles and the compare grip (a slider with min, max and current value).
- **Reflow:** no horizontal scroll at 640 px (a stand-in for 200% zoom) or 320 px on any of the three pages.
- **Forced colours:** the top of the Superior page renders legibly in Chromium's forced-colours mode (one screenshot reviewed).
- **Image text alternatives:** the lake-floor figure has a full description as its `alt`. My first check flagged its link as empty, which was a false alarm: the link takes its name from the image.
- **Climate Pair (source read only):** `climate/viz.js` sets the labels on its spiral canvases dynamically, gives its timeline strip keyboard arrow steps, and marks the layer readout as a table. That is a good pattern for the other pieces. It was not run.
- **Wrapper:** `superior-ice.qmd` gives its iframe a `title`.

## Not checked (and why)

- **No screen reader** (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver, TalkBack). All "announced" claims come from the accessibility tree, not from listening. A human test script would be a useful next deliverable.
- **No Safari, no real phone,** and no Firefox. Canvas `filter` on older Safari is known and untested.
- **The Quarto site was not built** (Quarto could not be installed in this environment). Not tested: the Tracks, Glossary, Methods, About and Home pages, the Climate Pair page as embedded, `dictionary/*.html`, the navbar and footer, and the iframe embedding, including focus movement between wrapper and piece.
- **Colour-blind simulation and non-text contrast (1.4.11)** for the maps, spirals and heat strips. Whether colour alone carries ice or temperature is unassessed. That is the largest open question about the pictures.
- **Text spacing and 200% text zoom** properly (I resized the viewport instead).
- **Reduced motion:** the rules exist (`shared/tokens.css`, and canvas checks on Superior and Water Year). I did not verify that the canvases stop, and Meridian has no canvas-level check of its own.
- **Audio itself:** no captions or transcript review, and no listening.
- **Automated tools find only part of the problem.** axe reported 3 to 5 failing rules per page (contrast, `lang`, landmarks, and on some pages an empty header or an unreachable scroll box); the largest findings above (A11Y-01, 02, 05, 11) came from reading the tree and the source, not from axe.

## Method and versions

- Chromium 141.0.7390.37 through `playwright-core` 1.63.0, `axe-core` 4.13.0, Node 22.22.2. axe tags: WCAG 2.0/2.1/2.2 A and AA plus best-practice.
- Pages were served from the repository by `dev/a11y/server.js` (a static server with HTTP range support) at 1280 and 390 px.
- Reproduce: `cd dev/a11y && npm i playwright-core axe-core`, then `node server.js &`, `node axe.js`, `node walk.js`, `node probe.js`, `node tree.js`. Details in `dev/a11y/README.md`.
- Console notes from the runs: `ERR_ABORTED` entries on the MP3 files are the browser cancelling range requests, not broken files. One 404 appeared in one run for a resource I did not identify (probably the browser's favicon request), and did not recur.
- Raw axe output: `dev/reports/a11y-audit-260930/axe-results.json`.
