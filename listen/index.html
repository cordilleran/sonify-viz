    How the harmony moves

    A mode is a set of seven notes. Arranged from dark to bright, each mode differs from its neighbour by exactly one note (in gold), so one step along the ladder is the smallest change of mood music can make. The season sets how bright the mode is. The year's departure from its 15-year normal nudges it one step darker or brighter. The mode can change only once every eight bars.

    Phrygian is not used. Its second note sits a half step above the home note and clashes under a sustained piano. The chord on top is written as a Roman numeral: upper case is major, lower case is minor. Each hydrologic phase (dormant, rising, peak, recession, low; or winter, filling, full, drawdown for the lake) has its own four-chord loop.

    What drives what

    Rings run from the centre outward in this order. R retrieved measurements. D derived from measurements or the calendar. S simulated timing. The simulated layers are modelled from temperature and season, not observed.
