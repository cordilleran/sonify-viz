Pressure: the low drone, the chords, and the deep drops

Triangles mark the bars where pressure moves 6 hPa or more in six hours. A fall brings a timpani hit, the tuba sinking and the Bear Sax (a baritone sax an octave below the chord root) sliding down with a sub drop under it; a rise brings a timpani roll and rising violins. In a fall of 3 hPa or more the sax holds the root and the drone's third becomes a fourth. The dotted lines at 3 and 6 hPa are compositional thresholds.

Wind: surface and 3 km up

10 m wind (conga density)700 hPa wind (cello pizzicato)

Directions are where the wind comes from, north at the top. The three shaded sectors decide which steps of the bar the congas accent: wind from 45° to 225° (labelled S/E) accents steps 1, 4, 7, 10 and 13; from 225° to 290° (W), steps 1, 5, 8, 11 and 14; from 290° through north to 45° (N), steps 1, 4, 8, 11 and 14. The sector labels only give three different patterns. The angle between the needles is the shear: under 60 degrees the harp counter-line is a third above, 60 to 120 a fifth, over 120 a seventh.

Temperatures near the ground and aloft

The song voices rain and snow from the model's own amounts. This plane shows the temperatures around them (the 850 hPa value is not heard): in the opening storm the ground is near 3 °C while 850 hPa, about 1.5 km up, is far below freezing.

Sun and the day

Each quarter of the ring is the six hours before its time stamp, which is the window the model's radiation and rain are totalled over. The model gives one radiation value per window. The song spreads it along that window's daylight curve (the sine of the sun's height, the same curve the Meridian Chorus uses, rounded at sunrise and sunset), scaled so the window keeps the model's value. The gold shape is that curve, and the best moment of each day sets how loud that day's pulse gets. The hand sweeps the clock time as the bar plays. Noon is at the top and midnight at the bottom, the way the sun climbs and sets.

Chords and mode

Air below 3 °C plays a dark minor loop in D Aeolian (i, VI, iv, VII); air at 3 °C or above plays a brighter D Dorian loop (i, IV, VII, IV). Pressure level drifts the loop up or down the scale. The pitched parts take their notes from the chord (the harp counter-line can add a seventh, and the drone's suspension adds a fourth), so everything stays inside one scale.

The input column says what the sound reads: R a value taken from the model run as published; D a value worked out from published values (a difference, a ratio, a threshold, a curve); A an assumption, open to change. Every mapping rule is the author's choice. hPa (hectopascals) is the unit of air pressure; 700 hPa is about 3 km up and 850 hPa about 1.5 km. 850 hPa temperature and the freezing level are shown for context and are not heard.

About this edition

Weather data: ECMWF AIFS Single forecast, run of 16 October 2026 (12 UTC), 6-hourly, taken from ECMWF’s open data and interpolated from the 0.25-degree grid to 49.21 N, 68.14 W, over the estuary about 45 km west of Godbout village (marked on the banner; the dashed box is about one 0.25-degree model grid box). Times are a fixed UTC-5 (Eastern Standard, with no daylight-saving change), not UTC. The model ends at 15 days, so the last storm is only two bars. It is a model, not an observation, and six-hour steps hide a front's passage. Snow in cm is the model’s snowfall, counted as one centimetre of snow per millimetre of water.

Forecast data © 2026 European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF), licensed under CC BY 4.0 (terms). Sea floor and land: GEBCO 2025 Grid (GEBCO Compilation Group, doi 10.5285/37c52e96-24ea-67ce-e063-7086abc05f29), public domain, not for navigation; the banner is a smoothed picture, not a chart.

Instruments: VSCO 2 Community Edition (CC0); Karoryfer Samples Big Rusty Drums, War Tuba and Bear Sax (CC0). Written and built with AI assistance (Claude Code); the groove and its mapping are the author's choices. Godbout lies on the north shore of the St Lawrence, in Nitassinan, the Innu homeland, within the traditional territory of the Innu of Pessamit.
